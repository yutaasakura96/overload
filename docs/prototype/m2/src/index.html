Overload M2: every screen, to correct before building

The phone is clickable and behaves like the app, on generated sample data: a cut in its seventh week, a food list of fourteen foods, sixty days of weigh-ins. It draws M2 whole: Today as the whole day (Q7), the new Food tab, Weight in Progress, the four M2 screens redrawn after the 2026-10-09 readability review, and every screen no artboard drew, with their error and empty states. It uses the app's own stylesheet, so the look is today's.

Click a button and it works. Click anything else, a line of text or a figure, to leave a comment on it.
