Overload: a prototype to correct before building

The phone is clickable: it behaves like the app, on a year of sample workouts. It covers the workout screen (#47), history (#48), the strength overview (#49), muscle balance (#50), full-screen mode with the iPhone Focus guide (#51) and the exercise filter (#41). It uses the app's own stylesheet, so the look is today's. Structure and wording are what is new.

Click a button and it works. Click anything else, a line of text or a figure, to leave a comment on it.
